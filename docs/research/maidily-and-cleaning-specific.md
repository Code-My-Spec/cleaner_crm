## 1. Maidily — Overview

Maidily positions itself as an all-in-one, **cleaning-native** platform: "built specifically for cleaning companies" rather than a generic field-service tool adapted to cleaning. Target customer is the small-to-midsize **residential cleaning / maid service** owner. Cloud-based, with a mobile app for field staff.

Sources: [maidily.com](https://www.maidily.com/), [pricing page](https://www.maidily.com/pricing.html)

### Core capabilities

| Area | What Maidily offers |
|---|---|
| **Online booking** | Branded, 24/7 online booking page; can be embedded on the client's own website; built to convert website-visitor leads |
| **Scheduling** | Drag-and-drop calendar "command center"; one-click maid assignment; one-click reminders |
| **Recurring jobs** | Automatically generates recurring cleanings; each occurrence counts as a separate job |
| **Client management** | Customer records, job notes, stored details; customer portal |
| **Quoting** | Professional quoting tool for fast estimates |
| **Invoicing** | Auto-generated invoices, custom line items, one-click send/preview |
| **Payments** | Online payments via Stripe; card storage; Square also supported |
| **Cleaner/staff mobile app** | Field-staff app: schedule access, customer details & job notes on-device, push notifications for new assignments, team messaging |
| **Communications** | Two-way SMS, automated email + text reminders, custom message templates; optional dedicated SMS number |
| **Team management** | Team calendar/scheduling, PTO tracking, time tracking, GPS/location tracking (top tier) — verify cleaners are on-site at clock-in |
| **Automations** | Automated customer reminders to cut last-minute cancellations; AI-powered scheduling (top tier) |
| **Integrations** | QuickBooks sync (customers/invoices/payments), Stripe, Square, Airbnb/VRBO (top tier), API access (top tier) |

**Cleaning-native angle:** the value proposition is that recurring-cleaning workflows, per-maid assignment, and cleaner reminders are first-class — versus horizontal FSM tools (Jobber, Housecall Pro) where recurring maid scheduling is bolted on. Notably, however, Maidily's own marketing does **not** detail cleaning checklists, photo documentation, or room-by-room SOPs — a gap relative to ZenMaid and MaidCentral (see below).

## 2. Maidily — Pricing (2026)

Job-metered model: you pay by monthly job volume, and **all tiers include unlimited users/team members**. "A job is any scheduled cleaning appointment. Recurring cleanings count as separate jobs each time they occur."

Source: [maidily.com/pricing.html](https://www.maidily.com/pricing.html) (direct fetch). Note: third-party listings (Capterra/GetApp) quote older "$30 / $60 / $100" Essentials/Power/Power+ tiers — the live pricing page below is current.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0/mo | 10 jobs/mo | — | No credit card required |
| **Start** | $29/mo | 50 jobs | +$3 / 50-job pack | Booking, scheduling, SMS, invoicing, payments |

- No contracts, cancel anytime; paid plans include free trials.

## 3. What cleaning businesses like about Maidily

Ratings are strong: **5.0/5 on Capterra** (~30 reviews) and positive on G2 ("powerful all-in-one software solution for the housecleaning industry").

- **Ease of use / intuitive design** — repeatedly cited; low learning curve.
- **Unlimited users on every plan** — no per-seat penalty as the team grows; a genuine differentiator vs. ZenMaid, Launch27, and BookingKoala, which all charge per seat or gate by plan.
- **Low entry price** — free tier + $29 starter is among the cheapest cleaning-native options; friendly to brand-new solo operators.
- **Online booking that converts** — branded/embeddable booking page is a headline draw.
- **Responsive customer service** (in positive reviews) — "top notch," responsive team.
- **Scales operations** — users report it helped them handle growth and reduce last-minute cancellations via automated reminders.

Sources: [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/201538/Maidily/reviews/), [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/maidily/reviews)

## 4. Where Maidily falls short (from reviews & feature gaps)

Do not over-index — overall sentiment is positive — but recurring complaints and gaps:

- **Bugs / stability** — a reviewer noted "too many bugs and not enough staff to fix issues." Suggests a small team stretched thin.
- **Customer service inconsistency** — despite many praising support, at least one review called it "atrocious" (slow responses, slow fixes). Support quality appears uneven.
- **Feature maturity** — some reviews referenced the **mobile app and customer portal as still in development** at review time; these now exist but the product reads as younger/thinner than incumbents.
- **No cleaning checklists / photo proof / quality scorecards** in the marketed feature set — ZenMaid (digital checklists, service ratings) and MaidCentral (customer scorecards, technician performance) are stronger on cleaning-quality workflows.
- **Higher-value ops features gated to top tier** — GPS tracking, AI scheduling, API, and Airbnb/VRBO are Scale-only ($99).
- **Payroll** — not clearly offered (ZenMaid and MaidCentral include payroll/payroll-grade reporting).
- **Reporting depth** — light compared to MaidCentral's KPI/analytics focus.

Sources: [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/201538/Maidily/reviews/), [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/maidily/reviews)

## 6. Gaps & opportunities for a new entrant (cleaner_crm)

Reading across the market:

1. **Cleaning-quality workflows are the real moat, and they're uneven.** Maidily under-serves checklists, photo proof, and quality scorecards; MaidCentral does it but only at $450+/mo. A mid-priced product with strong **room-by-room checklists, before/after photos, and customer quality scorecards** hits a real gap.
2. **Unlimited-users pricing is loved** (Maidily) but **per-seat pricing is the norm** (ZenMaid, BookingKoala, Launch27). A transparent, seat-friendly model is a differentiator.
3. **Support/stability is a recurring pain** for the cheap tools. Reliability + responsive support is a wedge against Maidily specifically.
4. **The $50–$150 mid-market is thin between "cheap-but-basic" (Maidily/ZenMaid) and "expensive-but-deep" (MaidCentral).** An operationally deep product (payroll, KPI reporting, automations) priced below MaidCentral is an opening.
5. **Recurring-revenue analytics** (churn, recurring vs. one-off mix, per-cleaner profitability) are largely MaidCentral-only — an opportunity to democratize.
6. **Cleaner mobile experience** (GPS, clock-in, SOS, on-site checklist completion) is the daily-use surface; getting it excellent is where boutique operators feel the product.
