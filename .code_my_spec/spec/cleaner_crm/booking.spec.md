# CleanerCrm.Booking

Public acquisition funnel: instant-quote calculation from the account's rate card (bedrooms/bathrooms/sqft/frequency/add-ons with frequency discounts), lead capture, and quote acceptance — slot selection, cancellation-policy agreement, Stripe card capture — orchestrating Clients (create client+property), Scheduling (create plan/job), Quoting (rate card pricing), and Billing (card on file).
